# Annotation and evaluation plan

Version 0.1.0 contains unannotated images of 5 objects: 3 Middle Kingdom and 2 New
Kingdom. Period labels do not establish the language stage of every inscription.

Current transcription, transliteration and translation fields are null. Empty
sign_annotations means no signs have yet been labeled, not that the image has no signs.

Next: choose inscription-bearing views, locate exact scholarly readings and their
reuse terms, annotate sign regions/identities/grouping/reading order, record damage
and disagreements, then attach cited translations with independent rights records.

All photographs, crops and augmentations of the same object share its split_group.
No photographs of an object may cross training and evaluation partitions. This
starter has no independently photographed test set and no accuracy claims.

Known-object retrieval and unfamiliar-text translation must be evaluated separately.
