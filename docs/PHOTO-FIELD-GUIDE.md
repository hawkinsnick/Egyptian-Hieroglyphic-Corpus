# Art Institute photo checklist

These are our proposed capture specifications, not permission from the museum.
Online collection work continues whether or not you visit.

## Before a future visit

Aim for **5 objects × 12 still photographs = 60 files**. One file per object is a
label reference; 55 are object/inscription photographs. Fewer good photographs are
better than rushed, blurred ones. Skip objects if crowded, restricted, or not on view.

First confirm the current visitor rules at https://www.artic.edu/visit/faqs and
ask staff whether the intended public research dataset, redistribution, and model
training are permitted. Personal photography permission does not establish public
dataset permission. The institution's online CC0 downloads are a separate route.
Obtain written clarification before treating visitor photos as publicly distributable.
No museum contact has been made by this project.

The museum's published summer/fall 2025 visitor guide excludes flash, video,
tripods, and selfie sticks and notes object-specific photography restrictions.
That dated guide is background, not a guarantee of the rules on your visit date:
https://www.artic.edu/files/0a6abda1-6777-42d9-ad23-7036e069e4ec/VisitorGuide_English_summer-fall-25.pdf
Use a handheld phone, existing light, still photos, and follow current signs and
staff instructions. Do not bring lights, move objects, touch cases, cross barriers,
or obstruct other visitors. Photograph an accessible label only if allowed; record
the accession number manually if not.

## The 12-shot recipe for each object

| Shot | Capture | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Museum label, legible accession number | Identify the exact object; internal reference, not training imagery |
| 02 | Whole object, straight on, medium distance | Main independent camera query |
| 03 | Whole object, straight on, farther back | Different scale and some surrounding context |
| 04 | Whole object, straight on, filling most of frame | More sign detail without cutting off the object |
| 05–06 | First readable inscription region, straight on then slightly oblique | Close detail and a second viewpoint |
| 07–08 | Second region, straight on then slightly oblique | A different passage |
| 09–10 | Third region, straight on then slightly oblique | A different passage |
| 11 | Whole object from modestly left, if accessible | Perspective variation |
| 12 | Whole object from modestly right, if accessible | Perspective variation |

A slight angle means roughly 10–20 degrees as a capture suggestion, not a reason
to move into a restricted area. If there are fewer than three regions, use the
available regions at different distances and note this. If sides are inaccessible,
substitute additional frontal views. Record what you actually captured.
For adjacent close-ups, include approximately 25–30% overlap where possible.

## iPhone capture settings and quality

- Use ordinary still Photo mode and the main camera at 1×. Avoid digital zoom;
  move only within permitted visitor space. An optical lens is optional.
- JPEG (.jpg/.jpeg) is easiest for the future pipeline. Original HEIC (.heic) is
  also acceptable; preserve it and make a separate JPEG working copy later.
- Keep the camera's original resolution; aim for at least 12 megapixels when
  available. There is no need for RAW, ProRAW, 48 MP, a special camera, or video.
- Turn flash off. Avoid Portrait mode, filters, markup, screenshots, and edits.
- Hold steady, tap the inscription to focus, and check a close-up before moving on.
  If available glyphs look soft when enlarged, retake rather than sharpen artificially.
- Keep the inscription plane as square to the camera as practical. To reduce glass
  reflections, change position slightly; do not press the phone against the case.
- Do not deliberately photograph people. Wait, reframe, or skip; review accidental
  faces before any public release. Preserve originals privately, not in a public repo.

## File handoff

Keep original filenames and original files. Organize copies into one folder per
accession number, for example `artic_1920.262/`. Add a manifest mapping filenames to
shot numbers. Do not rename your only originals.

Transfer using an original-file export to Files/AirDrop or equivalent. Do not send
screenshots, social-media downloads, or messaging-app compressed copies. ZIP the
folders for upload. HEIC files can be converted locally later; conversion must not
replace originals, and metadata removal for publication creates a labeled derivative.

Record institution, accession number, title, capture date, phone model, original
filename, shot number/type, reflections/blur/occlusion, and permission status. Do not
infer phone model or permission from absent metadata. Record gallery if known;
gallery location is not a permanent object identifier.

Use [photo-capture-template.csv](photo-capture-template.csv) for the first 12 shots.
Label photographs are identification aids: do not OCR their modern prose into the
public corpus without a separate reuse assessment. Museum online CC0 licenses do
not automatically apply to visitor photographs or label text.

## If staff ask what you are doing

Explain accurately that you are collecting handheld still photos for an independent
hieroglyph recognition research project and ask what is allowed, including public
sharing. Do not describe the project as museum-sponsored or hide the intended use.
A staff member's informal okay to take a photo is not a written redistribution grant.

If asked to stop, stop and ask calmly for a supervisor or visitor-services help.
Do not fight, resist restraints, or try to escape. A photo dataset can wait.

## How we will use the photographs

First for private known-object retrieval testing, subject to applicable permissions.
For that task the reference and query intentionally depict the same object, but
must be separately captured images. Report this as retrieval, not novel-sign OCR.

For learning to read unseen inscriptions, keep each object and all its views/crops
in the same train/validation/test group. Real camera queries must be distinguished
from transformed museum images. Unchecked sign labels remain provisional.
